Safeguards

Controls built intothe coin itself.

AeroCoin's rules are written into its smart contract on Base, so they apply to every coin, every time.

Daily issuing limit

New credit can only be created up to a set amount each day.

Total credit cap

The amount of unspent credit outstanding at any time has a fixed ceiling.

Two-person approval

Changes to AeroCoin's settings need sign-off from more than one keyholder.

Emergency pause

If something looks wrong, activity can be paused while it's resolved.

Lost-wallet recovery

If you lose access to your wallet, your balance can be moved to a new one after we verify it's you.3

Retired when spent

Every coin spent is destroyed on the spot, so credit can never be used twice.

The basics

What is AeroCoin?

AeroCoin is digital store credit issued by AeroAssist Industries. You earn it, hold it in a wallet on the Base network, and spend it on products and services from AeroAssist Industries or AeroHome.

What is one coin worth?

One AeroCoin is always $1.00 of credit at AeroAssist or AeroHome. The value is set by AeroAssist and does not rise or fall.1

Who issues AeroCoin?

AeroAssist Industries, the Phoenix company that builds drone-as-first-responder aircraft. AeroHome, AeroAssist's home-security sister company, accepts AeroCoin at the same value.

Is AeroCoin a cryptocurrency like Bitcoin?

It uses the same kind of technology, a public blockchain, but it works very differently. Bitcoin's price moves with the market; AeroCoin is always $1.00 of credit, is issued only by AeroAssist, and is meant to be spent at two companies.

Is AeroCoin an investment?

No. AeroCoin is store credit for buying products and services. It gives you no ownership in, or share of the profits of, AeroAssist Industries or AeroHome, and AeroAssist does not buy it back or list it for trading.

Earning and spending

How do I get AeroCoin?

You earn it on purchases, through the AeroAssist rewards program and promotions, or receive it from another member's wallet.

How do I spend AeroCoin?

At checkout with AeroAssist or AeroHome, choose AeroCoin and approve the payment from your wallet. The coins you spend are retired on the spot, and the purchase is recorded against your order.

Can I spend part of my balance?

Yes. AeroCoin divides down to one cent, so you can apply any amount up to your balance.

Can I use AeroCoin at other businesses?

No. AeroCoin is accepted only by AeroAssist Industries and AeroHome.

Can I turn AeroCoin into cash?

No. AeroCoin is spent on products and services and has no cash value, except where the law requires otherwise.1

Wallets and transfers

What wallet do I need?

Any wallet that supports the Base network, such as Coinbase Wallet or MetaMask. Keep your recovery phrase offline and never share it with anyone, including AeroAssist.

How do I add AeroCoin to my wallet?

Add it as a custom token on the Base network using the details in the Wallet section above, and your AeroCoin balance appears next to your other assets.

Can I send AeroCoin to someone?

Yes, any amount, to anyone with a wallet on Base. Double-check the address before you send: transfers on a blockchain can't be undone.2

Are there any fees?

AeroAssist doesn't charge you to earn, hold, send or spend AeroCoin. The Base network charges a small fee for each transaction, paid in ETH from your wallet; that fee is set by the network, not by AeroAssist.2

What if I send AeroCoin to the wrong address?

Blockchain transfers are final, so the coins go to whoever controls that address. AeroAssist may be unable to recover them, so always check the address first.2

What if I lose access to my wallet?

Contact AeroAssist. After we verify your identity, we can move your full balance to a new wallet you control.3

Safety and legal

Is AeroCoin insured?

No. AeroCoin is store credit, not a bank deposit, and is not insured by the FDIC or any government agency. AeroAssist Industries is not a bank.

How is AeroCoin protected?

Its rules live in its smart contract: a daily limit on new credit, a cap on total unspent credit, two-person approval for any setting change, an emergency pause, and retirement of every coin the moment it's spent.

Can I buy or sell AeroCoin on an exchange?

AeroAssist doesn't list AeroCoin on any exchange or buy it back. If someone else offers to buy or sell it, that price isn't set, endorsed or honored by AeroAssist; AeroCoin is always worth $1.00 of credit with us.1

Where can I see AeroCoin on the blockchain?

AeroCoin runs on the Base network, so every coin issued, sent and spent is publicly visible on Basescan, Base's block explorer.

Can the AeroCoin program change?

AeroAssist Industries may change, suspend or end the program, subject to applicable law. Changes are posted on this site.
