    Your coins sit in your wallet on Base. Check your balance anytime, or send coins to family and friends.

    Use them at checkout with AeroAssist or AeroHome. Each coin takes $1.00 off and is retired once spent.

Questions

Something else on your mind? Ask us through the chat on aeroassist.us.

What is AeroCoin?

AeroCoin is store credit issued by AeroAssist Industries. You earn it, hold it in a wallet, and spend it on products and services from AeroAssist or AeroHome.

What is one coin worth?

Always $1.00 of credit at AeroAssist or AeroHome. The value is fixed and set by AeroAssist; it doesn't rise or fall.

How do I get AeroCoin?

You earn it on purchases and through the AeroAssist rewards program and promotions, you can buy credit at face value, and other members can send you coins.

Can I send AeroCoin to someone?

Yes. You can send coins to anyone with a wallet on Base, for example to share credit with family.

Can I turn AeroCoin into cash?

No. AeroCoin is spent on products and services at AeroAssist and AeroHome; it isn't exchanged for cash.

What wallet do I need?

Any wallet that supports the Base network, such as Coinbase Wallet or MetaMask. Every coin issued and spent is recorded publicly on Base.
